SECMS - Validation Rules

1. Student_Email_Domain_Validation

Purpose: Validates that the student’s email uses the required
institutional domain.

Formula: NOT(CONTAINS(Email__c, “@student.edu”))

Error Message: Email must be a valid student institutional email.

2. Fees_Must_Be_Paid_Before_Approval

Purpose: Prevents an enrollment from being approved when fees have not
been paid.

Formula: AND( ISPICKVAL(Enrollment_Status__c, “Approved”), Fees_Paid__c
= FALSE )

Error Message: Fees must be paid before approving an enrollment.
