SECMS - Approval Process

Approval Process Name: Approval_Enrollment_Request

Purpose: Manages approval of enrollment requests.

Entry Criteria: Enrollment_Status__c = “Requested”

Approver: Training Manager

Final Approval Action: - Enrollment status is set to Approved.

Final Rejection Action: - Enrollment status is set to Rejected. -
Rejection email is sent.

Status: The approval process is activated in Salesforce.
